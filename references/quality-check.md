# Pre-delivery Quality Check

Use this checklist after drafting and before delivering any Suno package. Treat every musical setting and outcome as a creative recommendation, never as an official or guaranteed control.

## Lyric and musical coherence

- **Theme and movement:** State the premise, speaker, and intended emotional movement. Check that each verse introduces a new scene, action, consequence, or realization; do not restate the theme.
- **Hook and chorus:** Give the chorus one concise, memorable central phrase. It must answer the verse with a specific choice, image, or turn, rather than generic encouragement or filler.
- **Natural language and singability:** Read lyrics aloud in their intended groove. Keep wording conversational, stresses natural, line density breathable, and rhyme subordinate to meaning.
- **Labels and structure:** Use coherent functional labels when lyrics need them, and ensure the selected structure has a beginning, contrast, payoff, and an ending appropriate to the requested duration.
- **A/B differentiation:** For two versions, make Version B change a structure template or lead genre/arrangement axis. State the changed axis; cosmetic wording or accent-instrument swaps do not qualify.
- **Prompt and lyric alignment:** Confirm the English Style Prompt’s genre, groove, instrumentation, vocal treatment, structure/dynamic arc, mix, and emotional tone support the lyrics or instrumental roadmap.
- **Exclusions and requirements:** Keep Exclude Styles short and audible. Check it does not contradict the requested lead genre, vocal treatment, arrangement, or any required trait.

## Safety and request handling

- **Named artists and songs:** Translate named references into audible traits. Remove artist and song names from the final English Style Prompt and Exclude Styles; do not promise imitation of a person or voice.
- **Unsupported claims:** Describe duration, tempo, tonal tendency, arrangement, and other Suno-facing details as recommendations, not guarantees or official controls.
- **Version resilience:** Reject fixed-version syntax, parameters, UI labels, or feature dependencies (for example, a required model version, an undocumented toggle, or an exact field name). Describe only portable creative intent unless the user supplies a verified, current platform constraint.
- **Instrumentals:** Set language and perspective to `N/A`, vocal to `instrumental/no vocals`, and provide an arrangement-led instrumental structure. Do not add lyrics, sung hooks, or vocal recommendations.

## Revision priorities

Fix issues in this order:

```text
1. brief compliance
2. hook and emotional arc
3. language naturalness and singability
4. section development
5. prompt/arrangement alignment
6. polish and novelty
```

If a higher-priority repair changes the premise, structure, lead genre, or vocal role, repeat the downstream checks before delivery.
